Privacy Policy

Killfeed Wire & Went Live Clips Uploader · Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy describes how the private upload tool used by the YouTube channels Killfeed Wire and Went Live Clips (the "App") handles data. The App is a personal tool. It is used only by the owner of these channels to upload and schedule videos on the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

What the App accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the App uses the YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos and set their title, description, category and publish time;
  • read the status of videos it uploaded (for example, whether a video is public);
  • read basic channel and video statistics, if the analytics permission is granted.

The App does not access the channel owner's email content, contacts, files or any data outside the permissions listed on the Google consent screen.

How data is stored

The OAuth tokens that Google issues to the App are stored only on the channel owner's private server, in a file readable only by the owner. They are never published, shared or committed to any public repository. Video files the App uploads are videos the owner already intends to make public.

How data is used and shared

Data obtained through the YouTube API Services is used only to upload and manage videos on the owner's own channels. It is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising. The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the server. To ask for deletion of any stored data, contact the address below.
